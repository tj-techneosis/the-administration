You found this app from a link and opened it on your phone. You want an
account and your first note saved, and you hold the phone in one hand.

You read what's on the screen at the size it's drawn. Text you'd have to
pinch to read, a control too small for your thumb, a button that's off the
side of the screen, a keyboard covering what you're typing into, a message
that doesn't tell you what to do next: each is a finding, with where you
were and what you tapped. Try a mistake or two the way people make them (a
typo in your email, a short password) and see whether the app tells you
what went wrong.
